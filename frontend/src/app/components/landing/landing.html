Your Safety Is Our Priority

Every practitioner on findtherapy.care is individually vetted before their profile goes live. We verify professional body registrations so you can search with confidence.

Registration Required

Practitioners must provide their professional body membership or registration number when signing up — whether HPCSA, SACSSP, ASCHP, CCSA, or others.

Manually Reviewed

Our team individually reviews every new practitioner before their profile becomes visible. No auto-approvals — real people checking real credentials.

Approved & Visible

Only after approval does a practitioner appear in search results. This means every provider you find on our platform has been checked and verified.
