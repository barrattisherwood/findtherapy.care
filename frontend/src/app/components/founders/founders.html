Frequently Asked Questions

What does "grandfathered pricing" mean?

Your R99/month rate is locked in permanently. Even as we raise prices in the future, you'll always pay R99/month as long as your subscription remains active.

When do I need to pay?

Not for 6 months! Your founders trial gives you full access for 6 months at no cost. No credit card is needed upfront. After your trial, you'll pay just R99/month via PayFast.

What is the Founding Supporter badge?

As a founding supporter, your profile gets an exclusive badge that shows clients you were among the first therapists to join findtherapy.care. It demonstrates your commitment to making therapy accessible.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Note that if you cancel and resubscribe later, you'll be on the standard pricing (R150/month).

What happens after all 50 spots are taken?

New therapists will sign up at our regular pricing: 2-month free trial followed by R150/month. That's why it's worth joining early!
